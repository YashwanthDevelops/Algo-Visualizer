# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students studying Foundations of Artificial Intelligence, exploring problems and preparing for coursework.

## Product Purpose

Configure problems, inspect recorded algorithm decisions, visualize graph questions, and compare algorithm results.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Vanilla HTML, CSS, ES modules, SVG, Web Workers, LocalStorage. Five labs: pathfinding, N-Queens, game AI, Sudoku, and timetabling. Question Visualizer supports manual graphs and optional Gemini image extraction through the existing API. Preserve hash routes, saved inputs, playback, comparisons, and history.

## Product Principles

- Teach through real algorithm execution and recorded decisions.
- Keep computation and playback independent.
- Preserve existing learning features during visual changes.
- Redesign one page at a time with the user's direction.
